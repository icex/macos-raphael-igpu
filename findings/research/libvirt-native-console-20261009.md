## Capture and teardown: scoped pass, one remaining race

The harness reports valid CORE_PROBE_PASS with no earliest failure. Critical
capture accepts its existing terminal-prefix policy after a fresh caught-up
snapshot acknowledgement; this is not a claim that every intermediate replay
was flawless. The native GPU recovery receipt is recovered and
`authorizes_launch=true`. Host remains alive, GPU stays vfio-pci, and the
sleep:idle development inhibitor remains active.

The outer shutdown receipt says exited-after-guest-request. However, the
container-local libvirt `terminal.json` is absent and lifecycle events end at
running. Journal records critical serial EOF and its ExecStopPost exact-CID
`docker stop --time 0` at12:07:07, before the controller persisted termination.
The guest-request outcome and valid GPU recovery do not prove natural controller
exit. Preserve the capture-fatal guard while fixing this bounded exit race.

The manager also warned that global capabilities lacked x86_64, although the
explicit emulator and live domain worked. A separate isolated reproduction in
candidate342 identifies inherited launcher PATH shim contamination and validates
sanitized daemon discovery. That fix was not applied during341d.

## Scope and next test

This is the first native accelerated macOS rendering/input result inside the
actual virt-manager console in this series. It is not full lifecycle, performance,
resize, VirtualBox, independent-host-boot, or general desktop qualification.
Next native cycle must preserve the libvirt terminal receipt and qualify the
capabilities fix, then measure visible frame-token delivery in the actual manager
buffer. No physical HDMI/audio rerun occurred here;330 retains that evidence.
